SL Gas Finder — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Dobi Apps (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates SL Gas Finder, a community-powered application for finding and reporting LPG availability in Sri Lanka. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app as a guest or registered user, including when advertisements are displayed.

1. Information we process

Account information. If you register or sign in, our authentication provider, Supabase, processes account identifiers and sign-in information required by your chosen authentication method. Guests can use supported features without registering.

Location information. When you grant location permission, the app uses your device location to display nearby gas availability, map results, and search areas. You may also select a location manually on the map. Locations attached to submitted reports may be stored and shown to other users.

Community reports. Information you submit may include gas supplier or type, cylinder counts, queue estimates, availability, selected location, display name, notes, timestamps, and accuracy votes.

Technical information. Supabase, Google AdMob, and other infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, device information, identifiers, diagnostics, and request logs to operate, secure, measure, and improve their services.

2. How we use information

We use information to operate the map, display nearby LPG reports, save and update community submissions, support sign-in, calculate community verification results, prevent abuse, maintain service reliability, and display and measure advertisements.

3. Publicly visible information

Gas availability reports, selected map locations, submitted display names and notes, timestamps, and community verification information may be visible to other app users. Do not include your phone number, home address, passwords, or other sensitive information in public notes.

4. Google AdMob advertising

SL Gas Finder uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display banner advertisements. Depending on device settings, region, consent choices, and Google's advertising configuration, AdMob may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers (where available), IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and potentially ad personalization.

Google's handling of information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required, we will request advertising consent and provide applicable privacy choices. You may also manage advertising privacy preferences through your device's settings. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove advertisements.

5. Data storage and service providers

We use Supabase for database and authentication services and Google AdMob for advertising. Information sent to these providers is processed according to the services configured for this app and their applicable terms and privacy practices. Information may also be processed by network and map providers needed to display app features. We do not sell personal information directly to data brokers.

6. Location choices

You can control location permission through your device settings. If you decline permission, location-based features may be limited, but manual map selection may remain available. Locations attached to submitted reports may remain visible until those reports are removed.

7. Data retention

Gas availability reports are designed to be time-sensitive and may expire or be hidden according to app rules and community verification. Expiration from the map does not necessarily mean immediate deletion from database records or backups. Account information may remain until account deletion is completed, subject to necessary security, legal, or operational retention. Third-party providers may retain data under their own policies.

8. Account and data deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and associated personal information by emailing dobiappssl@gmail.com with the subject “SL Gas Finder Data Deletion Request”. If you used the app as a guest, include enough details about the report (such as approximate location and date) to help us identify it, but do not send passwords. We may need to verify ownership before fulfilling requests.

9. Security

We use service-provider safeguards and access controls intended to protect information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

SL Gas Finder is intended as a general community utility, not specifically directed to children. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, data handling, advertising practices, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
