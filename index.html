Privacy Policy SL Gas Finder · Dobi Apps Effective date: November 1, 2026 Dobi Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates SL Gas Finder, a community-powered application for finding and reporting LPG availability in Sri Lanka. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app as a guest or registered user. 1. Information we process Account information. If you register or sign in, our authentication provider, Supabase, processes the account identifiers and sign-in information required by the chosen authentication method. Guests can use supported features without registering. Location information. When you grant location permission, the app uses your device location to display nearby gas availability, map results and search areas. You may also select a location manually on the map. Community reports. Information you submit may include a gas supplier or type, cylinder counts, queue estimates, availability, location, notes, timestamps and accuracy votes. Technical information. Supabase and other infrastructure used by the app may process network and device-related technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to operate and protect the service. 2. How we use information We use information to operate the map, display nearby LPG reports, save and update community submissions, support sign-in, calculate community verification results, prevent abuse and maintain service reliability. We do not treat a guest submission as necessarily anonymous. 3. Publicly visible information Gas availability reports, their selected map locations, submitted notes, timestamps and community verification information may be visible to other app users. Do not include your name, phone number, home address or other sensitive information in public notes. 4. Data storage and service providers We use Supabase for database and authentication services. Data sent to Supabase is processed according to the services configured for this app and Supabase's applicable terms and privacy practices. Information may also be processed by network and map providers needed to display the app's features. We do not sell personal information. 5. Location choices You can control location permission through your device settings. If you decline permission, location-based features may be limited, but map-based manual selection may still be available. Locations attached to reports may remain visible until those reports are removed. 6. Data retention Gas availability reports are designed to be time-sensitive and may expire or be hidden based on app rules and community verification. Expiration from the map does not necessarily mean immediate deletion from database records or backups. Account information may remain until account deletion is completed, subject to necessary security, legal or operational retention. 7. Account and data deletion You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and associated personal information by emailing dobiappssl@gmail.com with the subject “SL Gas Finder Data Deletion Request.” If you used the app as a guest, include enough details about the report (such as approximate location and date) to help us identify it, but do not send passwords. We may need to verify ownership before fulfilling requests. 8. Security We use service-provider safeguards and access controls intended to protect information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. 9. Children SL Gas Finder is intended as a general community utility, not specifically directed to children. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed. 10. Changes to this policy We may update this policy when app features, data handling or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. 11.
